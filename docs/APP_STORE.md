# App Store listing (draft)

The listing copy for **Train**, decided in [ADR 2](adrs/2-rebrand-to-train.md) and finalized in #11 Phase 4. Only claim what has shipped. Check the "Bring your data" list against the app before every submission.

## Name and subtitle

- **Name:** Train: Built to Endure
- **Subtitle:** Cardio + strength, all in one

## Promotional text (170 characters max; editable without a new build)

> Free forever. Every feature, no paywall. Plan and track cardio + strength, and own your data.

## Keywords (100 characters max)

```
hybrid,running,workout,lifting,weightlifting,gym,endurance,tracker,log,planner,marathon,cycling
```

95 characters. Words already in the name or subtitle (train, built, endure, cardio, strength) are left out, because Apple ignores repeats. Other companies' trademarks (Strava, Garmin and so on) are never allowed here (Guideline 2.3.7).

## Description

> **Run and lift, side by side, for the long haul.**
>
> Train is built on one belief: consistent training in both cardio *and* strength keeps you healthier, less injury-prone and performing better much later in life. So both get equal billing. Plan them together, record them fast, and watch them add up.
>
> **Free forever.** Every feature is free, and always will be. If you choose to pay, it's only to support the creator or to remove ads, never to unlock features.
>
> **Your data, from anywhere, and it stays yours.**
> - Bring in workouts from Apple Watch, plus apps that save to Apple Health, such as Strava and Garmin Connect
> - Import and export CSV whenever you like
> - Keep a live copy in your own Google Sheet
> - Everything is stored on your device. No account, no lock-in.
>
> **Cardio**
> - Plan runs, rides and other sessions on a weekly calendar, including recurring plans
> - Record a session in seconds, with how it felt and how hard it was
>
> **Strength**
> - Lay out your lifts on a weekly board
> - Log a session quickly with one weight per lift
>
> **On-device AI coach**
> - Ask about your training by typing or speaking
> - The model runs entirely on your iPhone, so your conversations never leave it
>
> Privacy policy: https://bradlet.com/train/privacy/
> Terms of service: https://bradlet.com/train/terms/
